A Photon's Adventure

Follow what can happen to light inside the Sun today. Predict first, check your reasoning, reveal what happens, then choose the best explanation.

The four journeys are selected possible histories at four places in the Sun today. They are not a simulation of the most probable path of any particular photon, and moving between regions does not mean the Sun is changing over time. Temperature and density come from the standard solar Model S (Christensen-Dalsgaard et al. 1996). The wavelengths are illustrative choices, not random draws.
