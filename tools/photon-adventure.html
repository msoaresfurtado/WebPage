Explore further: photon energies, the spectrum, and physics notes

The three journeys are selected possible histories at three places in the Sun today. They are not a simulation of the most probable path of any particular photon, and moving between regions does not mean the Sun is changing over time. Temperature and density come from the standard solar Model S (Christensen-Dalsgaard et al. 1996). The wavelengths are illustrative choices, not random draws.
