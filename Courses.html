Courses

Since starting my tenure-track position in Fall 2024, I have taught two distinct courses at UW–Madison. In Spring 2027, I will co-teach Physics 109 — Physics in the Arts with Prof. Pupa Gilbert.

Astronomy 310 — Stellar Astrophysics

Intermediate · Physical Science

An intermediate course on the properties, structure, and evolution of stars. Topics include stellar atmospheres and radiative transfer, the equations of stellar structure, nuclear energy generation, and evolution from the main sequence to white dwarfs, neutron stars, and black holes.

Course website

Astronomy 104 — Our Exploration of the Solar System

Introductory · Physical Science

An introductory survey of the Solar System for non-science majors. Topics include the Sun, planets and moons, small bodies, planetary formation, and the search for habitable environments and life beyond Earth. We explore the physical principles behind planetary worlds and what observations and spacecraft missions reveal about them.

Physics 109 — Physics in the Arts

Introductory · Physical Science · Spring 2027

An introductory exploration of the physics behind music and visual art. Topics include sound and perception, harmony, musical scales and instruments, light and lenses, photography, and color perception and mixing.

Co-taught with Prof. Pupa Gilbert in Spring 2027.
